Clear, definitive answers to the most common engineering and operational questions asked by CTOs and founders.

What is a 30-Day Autonomous Engineering Sprint? expand_more
A 30-Day Autonomous Engineering Sprint is our flagship fixed-scope engagement. Over 30 calendar days, we architect and deploy a production-grade digital storefront, dual-rail billing engine (Paymob + Stripe), automated courier logistics (Bosta), and custom conversational AI agents. Every sprint follows our Stage-Gate Review Lifecycle, includes complete GitHub repository transfer, and comes with a 30-day post-launch bug-free technical warranty.
How does the Dual-Rail Billing Engine handle Paymob, InstaPay, and Stripe? expand_more
Our architecture dynamically determines customer geolocation and currency preference. Domestic Middle Eastern customers are routed through Paymob with native support for InstaPay, Meeza cards, mobile digital wallets (Vodafone Cash, Orange, Etisalat), and Cash on Delivery (COD). Global transactions route seamlessly through Stripe or Merchant of Record (MoR) rails. Both rails reconcile into an automated real-time accounting ledger.
How does Conversational Commerce work on WhatsApp and Instagram? expand_more
Rather than forcing social followers into a slow, clunky browser checkout, our conversational checkout engine allows customers to browse catalogs, select products, and complete payments or confirm COD directly inside WhatsApp Business or Instagram DMs in under three touches. Webhooks instantly sync the order with your inventory and courier APIs.
Who owns the source code and infrastructure after launch? expand_more
You do, 100%. On Day 30, we execute an institutional intellectual property transfer. We transfer GitHub repositories, Cloudflare Workers/Vercel projects, database credentials, and API connections directly into your enterprise accounts. You receive full ownership with zero recurring agency software licensing lock-in.
What security and privacy standards govern your architecture? expand_more
Every deployment complies with SOC 2 Type II trust criteria, ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS protocols, Egyptian Personal Data Protection Law No. 151/2020, European GDPR, and PCI-DSS Level 1 cardholder tokenization. Furthermore, all AI pipelines are red-team evaluated with Promptfoo against the OWASP LLM Top 10 vulnerabilities.
