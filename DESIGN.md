## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a high-performance, autonomous architecture. It is built for a digital product agency focusing on AI systems and high-throughput engineering. The aesthetic is clear, engineering-grade, and professional, minimizing fluff in favor of precision.

## Colors
- **Primary Graphite**: `#111111` - The base surface environment.
- **Accent Electric Blue**: `#007BFF` - The primary active state, CTA button, and high-signal action color.
- **Surface Slate Gray**: `#2A2A2A` - Used for structural panels, cards, and elevation layer 1.
- **Text Off-White**: `#F4F4F5` - Primary readability color.

## Typography
- **Inter**: Bold headers and body copy. Delivers uncompromised legibility for large-scale architectural narratives.
- **Roboto Mono**: Data, Pricing, Legal, and metadata badges.

## Layout & Components
- **Layout**: 12-column engineering grid on desktop, 4-column on mobile. Strict 8px spacing system.
- **Cards**: Slate Gray background with precise border definitions. On hover, Electric Blue border triggers to simulate active tracking.
- **Buttons**: Electric Blue background with Off-White text.
